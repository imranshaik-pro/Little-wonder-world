# Little Wonder World — Product Roadmap

Status: planned unless explicitly marked implemented. Preserve ideas here so they are not forgotten.

## Product principles
- Quality over activity count: pilot 15 excellent interactive activities, test with families, expand toward 30–60 distinct mechanics only after evidence.
- Fully offline Android experience for ages 3–8, with age-appropriate difficulty and clear parent controls.
- Realistic, scientifically accurate animal education, not passive video watching.
- Support English, Telugu, and Hindi.
- Child privacy by design: local-first, no unnecessary identifiers, parent consent for research.

## Animal Discovery
- Complete 64-animal library.
- Produce 58 missing movement clips, 16:9 landscape and 9:16 portrait; also create portrait equivalents for existing six.
- 8-second child-friendly clips, correct anatomy/habitats, real animal sounds with verified rights, no misleading audio.
- Watch → Listen → Learn → Play with habitats, food, recognition, and age-adjusted quizzes.
- Fit-center playback, offline media manifest, playback QA and file-size optimization.

## Little Wonder Life: parenting + daily habits
- Separate Kids World and Parent World.
- Short demonstration + parent guidance + real-world practice + celebration, without autoplay loops.
- Start with 12 high-value routines; later expand to 24.
- Hygiene: brushing, handwashing, bathing, tissue use, nails.
- Toilet independence: readiness, potty steps, accidents without shame, handwashing.
- Food/routines: new foods without pressure, water, meals, bedtime.
- Social-emotional: kindness, taking turns, asking politely, emotions, frustration, patience, repair.
- Safety and independence: road safety, helmets, body boundaries, asking for help, tidying up.
- Infant guidance is parent-facing rather than infant screen time.
- Pediatric/dental review of all health and safety instructions; verify AI videos show correct actions.
- Parent-entered offline progress, no labeling/shaming of children.

## Testing and launch
- Pilot 20–30 families across ages 3–4, 5–6, 7–8 with consent.
- Evaluate voluntary replay, completion, independent navigation, actual learning, parent usefulness.
- Accessibility, device orientation, APK performance, storage footprint, privacy, and offline testing.
- Android test APK first; production signing and Play Store review later.

2. Install and visually inspect on a real Android device.
3. Fix UX defects and create 5-animal dual-orientation media pilot.
4. Prototype one brushing lesson and one potty-readiness parent guide.
5. Test the first 15 activity designs before scaling.
