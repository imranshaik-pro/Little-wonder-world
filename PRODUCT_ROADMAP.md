# Little Wonder World — product ideas and implementation backlog

## Quality-first education
- Pilot 15 polished, repeatable activities; evaluate child enjoyment, independent use, learning gains and parent feedback before expanding toward 30–60 unique mechanics.
- Fully offline child-friendly app for ages 3–8 with age-adjusted difficulty; English, Telugu and Hindi.

## Animal Discovery
- 64 animals; generate remaining 58 movement videos in both 16:9 and 9:16, plus portrait versions of six existing clips.
- Authentic sounds, realistic habitats and anatomy; Watch → Listen → Learn → Play.
- Optimize video size, device orientation, offline playback and quality assurance.

## Little Wonder Life — parents and good habits
- Separate Kids World and Parent World.
- Watch → Practice in real life → Celebrate; short demonstration + parent guidance.
- Start with 12 lessons then expand to 24: toothbrushing, handwashing, toilet readiness and potty training, bathing, bedtime, mealtime, healthy food, kindness, sharing, emotional regulation, road safety, body boundaries and independence.
- Infant guidance is for parents to watch and practice, not for infant screen viewing.
- Pediatric/dental review of health instructions and verification of AI-generated demonstrations.
- Optional private local parent-entered progress, never shame children.

## Validation
- Pilot with 20–30 families in age bands 3–4, 5–6, 7–8 with consent; measure voluntary replays, completion, learning, independent navigation and usefulness.
- Test Android install, navigation, sound, offline media, performance and accessibility.
