# Little Wonder World 2.0 — Image Generation Master Prompts

## Production Methodology & Quality Standards
All images for Little Wonder World 2.0 are crafted using Google Gemini's advanced image synthesis architecture.
- **Resolution**: Native 1024 × 1024 pixels.
- **Export Format**: Optimized WebP (Lossy quality 88, Method 6 compression), maintaining lossless alpha transparency where isolated.
- **Style**: Ultra-realistic wildlife and product photography. Anatomically accurate, authentic lighting, no text, no watermarks, no decorative borders.
- **Visual Identity Persistence**: Animal features (e.g., ear shape, coat tint, tusk curvature) are strictly matched between still learning cards and video generation prompts.
